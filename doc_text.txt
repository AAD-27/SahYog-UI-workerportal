HOW SAHYOG should work?
Project Overview
Sahyog
 is a full-stack citizen welfare management system that helps identify and recommend welfare benefits based on an individual's financial, household, and personal circumstances.
The application follows a structured workflow similar to modern public assistance systems. Instead of asking citizens to fill separate forms for every welfare scheme, 
Sahyog
 collects information once and automatically evaluates eligibility for multiple programs.

Rule-Based Eligibility Engine
Phase 1 Scope
The initial version of 
Sahyog
 will focus on building a robust rule-based welfare eligibility platform. Citizens will register, provide detailed household and financial information, and receive transparent recommendations for five welfare programs—Food Coupons, Temporary Cash Assistance, Medical Insurance, Child Care Benefits, and Utility Bills Assistance. The emphasis is on accurate eligibility evaluation, modular microservices, and clear explanations for every recommendation, creating a strong foundation for future enhancements such as document verification, AI-assisted recommendations, and integration with government systems.
Programs:
The eligibility engine in 
Sahyog
 will follow a 
rule-based evaluation model
. Each welfare program will independently evaluate the citizen's profile, allowing an applicant to qualify for 
multiple programs simultaneously
. For example, a citizen may be eligible for 
FSP, MCARE, and EAP
 at the same time if they satisfy each program's criteria.
The only exception is 
CAP (Cash Assistance Program)
, which should have stricter eligibility because it provides direct financial assistance.
1. FSP – Food Support Program
Objective
Provide food assistance to households with limited financial capacity.
Eligibility Rules
Rule
Condition
FSP-01
Annual Household Income ≤ ₹3,00,000
FSP-02
Monthly Food & Grocery Expenses ≥ 20% of Monthly Income
FSP-03
Monthly Essential Expenses (Food + Utilities) ≥ 40% of Monthly Income
FSP-04
Applicant must be an Indian resident.
FSP-05
Applicant must not have an active FSP application under review or already approved.
Rationale
Food assistance should focus on financial need rather than household size. Even a single individual with very low income may require assistance.
2. CAP – Cash Assistance Program
Objective
Provide temporary financial assistance to citizens experiencing severe financial hardship.
This program has the strictest eligibility criteria because it involves direct cash transfers.
Eligibility Rules
Rule
Condition
CAP-01
Annual Household Income ≤ ₹2,00,000
CAP-02
Monthly Expenses ≥ 80% of Monthly Income
CAP-03
Applicant age between 18 and 60 years
CAP-04
Applicant must not own significant financial assets (Phase 2: asset verification).
CAP-05
Applicant must not already be receiving CAP benefits.
CAP-06
Applicant must satisfy at least one hardship condition: disability, major medical expenses, unemployment, or being the sole earning member.
Rationale
CAP is designed for citizens facing genuine financial distress and therefore combines income, expenditure, and hardship checks.
3. MCARE – Medical Care and Assistance for Residents
Objective
Provide medical insurance support to citizens requiring healthcare assistance.
Eligibility Rules
Rule
Condition
MCARE-01
Annual Household Income ≤ ₹5,00,000
MCARE-02
Applicant has a certified disability 
or
 a declared chronic/major medical condition.
MCARE-03
Monthly Medical Expenses ≥ 10% of Monthly Income.
MCARE-04
Applicant must be an Indian resident.
MCARE-05
Applicant must not already have an active MCARE application or approved coverage under the same program.
Rationale
Medical support should not depend on household size. Health-related need should be the primary driver of eligibility.
4. CAPS – Child Assistance and Protection Scheme
Objective
Provide assistance to households responsible for young children.
Eligibility Rules
Rule
Condition
CAPS-01
Household includes at least one child aged 5 years or younger.
CAPS-02
Annual Household Income ≤ ₹3,50,000
CAPS-03
Applicant is the parent or legal guardian of the child.
CAPS-04
Child resides in the applicant's household.
CAPS-05
Applicant must not already receive CAPS benefits for the same child.
Rationale
The program is child-focused, so eligibility depends on the presence of an eligible child rather than household size.
5. EAP – Energy Assistance Program
Objective
Assist households struggling to pay essential utility bills.
Eligibility Rules
Rule
Condition
EAP-01
Annual Household Income ≤ ₹3,00,000
EAP-02
Electricity and Water Bills together ≥ 15% of Monthly Income
EAP-03
Applicant is responsible for paying the utility bills (owner or tenant).
EAP-04
Applicant must be an Indian resident.
EAP-05
Applicant must not already have an active EAP application or approved benefit.
General Eligibility Principles
The eligibility engine should follow these principles:
Every welfare program is evaluated independently.
A citizen may qualify for multiple programs simultaneously.
Each rule is evaluated separately, and the outcome of every rule is stored.
The system provides a transparent explanation for every approval or rejection.
Threshold values (income limits, expense percentages, etc.) should be configurable through the database rather than hardcoded in the application.
This approach closely resembles real-world public benefit systems, where a single citizen profile is assessed independently against multiple program-specific rule sets. It also gives you flexibility to introduce new programs or modify eligibility thresholds without changing application logic.
